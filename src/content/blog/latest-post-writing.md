# Writing Posts That Read Quickly

Short posts are easier to scan, easier to maintain, and easier to keep visually balanced in a card grid.

## Structure

I usually aim for three parts:

- a clear idea in the first paragraph
- a short middle section with one or two headings
- a direct closing note

## Editing rule

If a paragraph doesn’t add new information, it probably belongs in the next draft instead.

## Outcome

This keeps the blog usable for both quick reading and longer follow-up pages.
