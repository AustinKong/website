## Overview

Atto is a local-first AI job-search assistant for tracking applications, parsing job listings, and tailoring resumes. It has received more than 500 downloads.

## What I worked on

I built the full-stack application with React, TypeScript, FastAPI, Python, and SQLite, keeping user data local while supporting AI-assisted workflows.
