## Overview

Finconnect won first place at the Visa x SFYC hackathon. The prototype lets affluent travelers deposit stablecoins, earn simulated staking yield, and explore global card spending.

## What I worked on

I developed the Express backend and integrated the prototype with Visa APIs.
