## Overview

FetchIT centralizes medical records, adoption workflows, and dog profiles for Action for Singapore Dogs.

## What I worked on

I built and deployed the platform with React and Supabase as part of Google Developer Groups NUS, helping the shelter manage its operational information in one place.
